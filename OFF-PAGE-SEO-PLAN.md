# CSS Service Solution — Off-Page SEO Plan

Off-page SEO cannot be completed by website code. Backlinks, citations, mentions, reviews, partnerships and digital PR must come from real external websites or profiles. Google does not publish a required backlink count; relevance, trust and natural acquisition matter more than volume.

## Recommended external link sources
1. **Google Business Profile** — keep the real business name, phone, category, service areas and website URL consistent with the real-world business.
2. **Bing Places** and other major business profiles — use the same genuine business data.
3. **Established local/industry directories** — only where the listing is genuinely useful; avoid bulk directory packages.
4. **Genuine client/vendor/association pages** — request a listing where a real relationship exists.
5. **Local chambers/associations** — only genuine memberships.
6. **Local media / digital PR** — earn coverage for pest-control education, seasonal safety campaigns or community work.
7. **Useful resources** — publish original termite-prevention checklists, pre-construction termite guides, monsoon pest-prevention guides and commercial pest-control checklists that other sites can naturally cite.
8. **Legitimate social/company profiles** — useful for discovery and trust even when the links are not strong ranking signals.

## Natural anchor text
Prefer branded or descriptive anchors such as `CSS Service Solution`, `pest control service`, `termite treatment service`, or a natural local description. Do not build large numbers of identical keyword-rich anchors.

## Avoid link spam
- Buying or selling links for ranking purposes
- Automated backlink software
- PBN/private link networks
- Bulk low-quality directories
- Excessive reciprocal link exchanges
- Keyword-rich forum/comment links
- Mass guest-post campaigns whose main purpose is links
- Creating external pages solely to manufacture links

Google lists these patterns as link spam. Paid/sponsored links used for advertising can be qualified with `rel="nofollow"` or `rel="sponsored"` when appropriate.
